BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Questions ISPs often ask.

Does Daraja provide internet or Wi-Fi equipment?

No. You bring the internet connection, MikroTik router, Wi-Fi access points, and customer coverage. Daraja connects payment checkout to hotspot account provisioning.

Can I accept live M-Pesa payments today?

This build is configured for Safaricom Daraja sandbox testing only. Do not use it to collect real customer payments until a production integration has been implemented and reviewed.

Can I set speed limits or data caps on bundles?

Not in the current product. Bundles define price and access time. Configure network policies separately and make any speed or usage terms clear to customers.

What do I need to try the workflow?

A public HTTPS backend, a configured MikroTik with a working WAN and customer LAN, sandbox credentials, and a test device connected to your Wi-Fi.
