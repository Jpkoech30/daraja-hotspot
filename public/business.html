A STRAIGHTFORWARD CUSTOMER JOURNEY

From “connect” to online.

  1. 01

    You set up the service

    Prepare your internet connection, customer Wi-Fi, and MikroTik. Add your bundles and connect the router to your workspace.

  2. 02

    Your customer picks a bundle

    They join your Wi-Fi, choose an access duration, enter their M-Pesa number, and approve the STK prompt.

  3. 03

    The router provisions access

    After payment confirmation, the router creates the hotspot account and the portal attempts to sign the customer in.

YOUR NETWORK, YOUR BUSINESS

You stay in control of the service.

Daraja connects the payment and hotspot workflow. You provide the upstream internet, configure Wi-Fi coverage and router policies, set bundle terms, and support your customers.
